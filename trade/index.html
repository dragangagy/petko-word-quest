    How G-Lab Trade works

    The game
    • You start with $10,000 of virtual cash.
    • Buy and sell simulated assets to grow your equity.
    • Prices are simulated and keep moving even while the app is closed.
    • The same market plays out on every device, so you can compare runs with friends.
    Trading
    • Tap an asset to open its chart and order panel.
    • Orders are placed in dollars; quantity is computed at the current price.
    • Fractional quantities are allowed, like real crypto exchanges.
    • Every trade pays a 0.1% commission, so overtrading costs you.
    • Use 25% / 50% / Max to size orders from your cash or holdings.
    Assets
    • Stocks are calmer; crypto is wilder; DOGZ is pure chaos.
    • AURM (gold) barely moves - a place to park cash.
    • Every asset has its own personality: trend, volatility and rhythm.
    Scoring
    • Total P&L compares your equity with the $10,000 start.
    • Reset account starts a fresh run from $10,000.
    • Your history keeps the last 100 trades.
    Fair warning
    • This is a game with fictional assets and virtual money.
    • Nothing here is financial advice, and no real money is involved.

    Reset account?

    This clears your positions and history and restarts from $10,000. The market itself keeps running.
