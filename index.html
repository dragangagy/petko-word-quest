    Help

    Game rules
    • Guess the 5-letter word.
    • After each try, colors show letter status.
    • Green means the letter is correct and in the right place.
    • Yellow means the letter is in the word, but in another place.
    • Dark means the letter is not in the word.
    • Classic mode is practice: one word, 6 tries, no points.
    • Competitive mode is played once per day.
    • Levels go in order: 1, 2, 4, 8.
    • The next level opens only if every board is solved.
    • If a level is not solved, the tournament ends for that day.
    • Solved boards dim or collapse and no longer accept letters.
    • If you leave the app, an unfinished run is saved.
    Scoring
    • Level 1 awards +4 points.
    • Level 2 awards +8 points.
    • Level 4 awards +16 points.
    • Level 8 awards +32 points.
    • Each unused try awards +5 bonus points.
    • If a level is not solved, you get -2.
    • The top score number is started competitive runs / yearly total points.
    • The large number below is the best daily score in the year.
    Bonus play
    • Friday unlocks the special “Petko Friday”.
    • Competitive points are doubled on Friday as a bonus to the yearly score.
    • Daily score on Friday is not doubled; it stays the clean result for that day.
    • Classic mode on Friday counts toward a bonus: solve 5 classics in a row for +5 on the main score.
    • If you fail a classic game, the Friday streak resets to 0/5.
    • Saturday and Sunday unlock “Witch Hunt Weekend”.
    • On entry you can pick weekend avatars Z41-Z45. The chosen avatar lasts until the end of Sunday, then your previous avatar returns.
    • While a weekend avatar is active, you get +1 allowed challenge every 3 hours. Classic streaks and Competitive still unlock extra challenges.
    Challenge
    • A challenge has 6 boards and 11 total tries.
    • You start each day with 1 allowed challenge.
    • Competitive unlocks more challenges: level 1 gives +1, level 2 gives +2, level 4 gives +4, and level 8 gives +8.
    • Challenge count is shown as used / allowed. Clearing every level gives 16 challenges for that day.
    • When you challenge someone, the invite waits for accept and shows as a yellow card.
    • If the opponent does not accept within 6 hours, the invite expires and the slot returns.
    • When accepted, the card turns green and can be played within 24 hours.
    • Receiving challenges is unlimited.
    • Played challenge results show as blue cards and clear after midnight.
    • A challenge is played only after the opponent accepts the code or link.
    Profile
    • Open your profile by tapping the small avatar window near the top of the game.
    • In the profile you can change your player name and avatar.
    • Tap the avatar image to open avatar selection.
    • The link code lets you use the same profile on multiple devices.
    • When you link a profile, results, challenges, and medals stay tied to the same player.
    • Name changes are limited so leaderboards and challenges stay readable.
